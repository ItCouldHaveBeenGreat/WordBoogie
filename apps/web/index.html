How to play

Pick letters anywhere on the board to build an English word of at least two letters. Letters don’t have to touch. Each tile can be used once per word.

Your word captures neutral tiles and unprotected rival tiles. A tile is protected when every neighboring tile above, below, left, and right belongs to its owner. Protected tiles show a small diamond.

Once any player plays a word, nobody can use it again—even with different tiles. Shorter prefixes of played words are also blocked; longer extensions are allowed.

When every tile is claimed, or everyone passes in a row, the most tiles wins. Ties share the win.

Words use SCOWL American English, size 60 (78,659 entries), bundled for offline play. Dictionary source and license.
